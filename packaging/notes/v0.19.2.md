Upkeep: the phone app now follows Android 16's rules, and a newer library writes archives. Nothing changes in how Brevier reads.

**Android 16's rules on the phone.** The app now targets Android 16 (API level 36), as Google Play asks of every app and every update since August 2026. On Android 16 an app targeting it hears the back button and the back gesture in a new way, and an app that only listened the old way would stop hearing them. Brevier now takes them the new way on Android 13 and later, in the same order as before: back closes what stands over the page first — the menu, the shelf, the tabs, settings, find on page, the address bar, a selection — then steps back through the tab's pages, and from the first page puts Brevier away, or returns you to the app that sent the link.

**zip 9.** The library that writes a saved article as `.zip`, and exported notes with their pictures, is now version 9. The advisories against version 8 are all about reading and extracting archives, and Brevier only writes them, so no earlier release was at risk; still, the release no longer carries a version with known flaws.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak; updates from Brevier's repository), `brevier-0.19.2-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.19.2-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key; it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.19.2-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.19.2-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), the command line alone: `brevier-cli-x86_64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-aarch64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-macos.tar.gz` and `brevier-cli-windows-x86_64.zip`, and `SHA256SUMS`.
