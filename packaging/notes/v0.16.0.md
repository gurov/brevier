High contrast, a phone bar that steps out of the way while you read, and numbered lists that line up. Two of the three were asked for in the comments to the article about Brevier on Habr.

**High contrast.** A switch beside the dark theme, in the window and on the phone: black ink on white paper, or white on black in the dark theme, with links, muted text, code and the shelf darkened to match, so any text reads at 7:1 or better. Pictures with transparent parts are laid on white then. Until you touch the switch, Brevier follows the system's contrast setting: the appearance portal on Linux (GNOME and KDE set it), high-contrast mode on Windows, the contrast level on Android 14 and later. The ivory paper stays the default, and two of its shades that fell short of WCAG's 4.5:1 are darker now: muted text (4.4:1 before) and comments in code (3.4:1).

**On the phone, the bar slides away while you read.** It now lies over the article and moves out of the way as you read down, as far as your finger goes, and comes back as you read up; let go halfway and it settles to the nearer edge. The text never moves under your eyes. It stays put at the top and the end of a page, on short pages and Brevier's own, while you type an address, search, select text or write a note, and with TalkBack or Switch Access on. The rules are the ones Firefox for Android uses for its bar. Settings → Slide the bar away turns it off.

**Numbered lists line up.** The text of an item now starts where its wrapped lines and the code under it do; after "1." it used to stand a little to the right. Numbers stand in a column, so items 9 and 10 begin their text in one place. A page with lists that you were reading opens once at the start of the right section rather than at the exact line.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak; updates from Brevier's repository), `brevier-0.16.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.16.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key; it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.16.0-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.16.0-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), the command line alone: `brevier-cli-x86_64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-aarch64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-macos.tar.gz` and `brevier-cli-windows-x86_64.zip`, and `SHA256SUMS`.
