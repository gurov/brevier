Fixes for the notes margin of 0.19.0, in the desktop window. Updating is recommended there; the phone is unchanged.

**The window narrows again.** With notes beside the text, a wide window could not be made narrower than about 1,100 pixels, 1,260 with the shelf; a tiling window manager cut the notes and the header off instead. The margin no longer holds the window: make it narrower, and the notes move under their paragraphs; wider, and they come back beside the text.

**A page without notes stays in the middle.** 0.19.0 kept an empty margin beside every article in a wide window, so the column stood to the left. Now the column moves aside only when the page has a note, and the note you start on such a page opens in the margin, ready for your words.

**The shelf keeps its width.** A note under its paragraph came out a little wider than the column, and when the window then grew, the shelf took the extra room and remembered it as your choice, up to its widest. That no longer happens. If your shelf became wide in 0.19.0, drag the divider back once.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak; updates from Brevier's repository), `brevier-0.19.1-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.19.1-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key; it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.19.1-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.19.1-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), the command line alone: `brevier-cli-x86_64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-aarch64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-macos.tar.gz` and `brevier-cli-windows-x86_64.zip`, and `SHA256SUMS`.
