Pictures no longer bring Brevier down on Android 9 and earlier, the phone turns the page with a tap, a swipe or the volume keys, and bookmarked pages keep their pictures. Most of it came from readers: a letter from a reader on F-Droid, the testers of the Google Play closed test (#48) and the comments on the Habr article.

**Pictures on Android 9 and earlier (#58).** On an LG V30 with Android 9, any Ars Technica article with pictures closed Brevier, and every start after it too. Android 9 has a bug in the way it copies pixels into an image when they do not start at the beginning of their buffer, and Brevier's did not; newer versions fixed it, which is why the same pages were fine on Android 17. The pixels now start at the beginning. The crash also got past the guard of 0.21.0 (#57), because it happened inside Android rather than in Brevier; the guard now covers that too, so a page that brings Brevier down waits on the next start, whatever the cause.

**Turning the page (#55).** In Settings, under Turning the page, each with its own switch and all off: a tap on the text turns forward, on the left third back (**Left hand** swaps the sides); a swipe sideways, right to left forward; the volume keys, down forward and up back, while a page is open. A turn moves by a screen less one line, so the last line stays in sight, and stops between lines. Links, pictures and notes work as before, and past the end of a page Brevier offers the next one rather than going there.

**Pictures in the archive (#42).** A bookmarked page and a page with notes keep their pictures, next to their copy in the archive: the ones already downloaded to show the page, with no extra request, up to 32 MB a page. Brevier shows them from there before asking the site, so the pictures outlive it too. On the phone they stay out of the backup to Google, which stops backing an app up at all past 25 MB.

**Search is easier to find (#50).** While what you type is not an address, the first line under the address bar reads **Search DuckDuckGo for "…"**, and the empty bar says what it is for. On the phone the suggestions no longer cover the address bar while the keyboard is up.

**Front pages with regional versions (#49).** A front page such as `nsk.rbc.ru`, where a reader in Novosibirsk lands from rbc.ru, showed only its footer: Brevier took `rbc.ru` for a suffix like `co.uk` and counted the headlines on `www.rbc.ru` as another site's. Regional pages of aif.ru and mk.ru gain their headlines too.

**Your own User-Agent, for developers.** At the very bottom of Settings, in the window and on the phone. Brevier says `Brevier` unless you switch this on, and the setting says why we recommend keeping it: sites may show pages wrongly or not at all with another name.

**Windows (#61).** If the window closes before it appears — on some older laptops the graphics driver brings GTK down — the next start draws without the graphics card and says so at the bottom of the window. To try the graphics card again, delete `drawing.txt` in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Brevier`.

**Also:** the window no longer prints "Trying to measure BrevierArticle" warnings.
