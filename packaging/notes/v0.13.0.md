News front pages read as their headlines.

**A news site's front page reads as its headlines.** An article extractor looks for one story on a page, and it finds one even on a front page: the longest item, the lead block, a column of opinion. Brevier now collects a front page's headlines by the shape of their links — a link to the same site with headline-length text and an address that looks like an article, or a whole heading — and when what the extractor picked carries fewer than two thirds of them, the page reads as the list of headlines, in the page's own order. On 211 news front pages, the share of each site's newest headlines that reach the reader went from 42% to 95%. Only a site's front page, or the front of its language version such as `/ru/`, is read this way; section pages read as before.

**On crates.io.** `cargo install --locked brevier --features ui` builds the window and the command line, and `--features save` the command line alone. From this version on, every release reaches crates.io from CI, along with the files below.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak), `brevier-0.13.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.13.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key — it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.13.0-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.13.0-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), and `SHA256SUMS`.
