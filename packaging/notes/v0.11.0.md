Threads from Hacker News, Lobsters and Discourse, Rust documentation read whole, and a round of small fixes.

**Hacker News reads as threads.** A story opens with its link, site, points, author and day, then every comment with its author and its day, replies indented; a reply too deep to indent says whom it answers. Short replies that are mostly a link are no longer dropped. The front page and the other lists of stories — new, ask, show, jobs, past — are lists of links with a link to the comments, and `Space` at the end goes to the next page. A comment's own page names its story and its parent.

**Lobsters and Discourse too.** Lobsters threads read the same way, and a link to one comment (`lobste.rs/c/…`) opens the thread at that comment. Discourse forums recent enough to serve Markdown were already read as they serve it; older ones are now read from their page for clients without JavaScript, every reply with its author and day.

**Rust documentation reads whole.** Pages from docs.rs, the standard library and any rustdoc build come from rustdoc's own layout: signatures as highlighted code, every method and implementation in order, none of the buttons.

**Tables of labels and values keep their labels.** Wikipedia's infoboxes and MDN's summaries lost every row label; each row is now a line, **Developer:** The Rust Team. In other tables a row label becomes its first cell.

**Smaller things.**
- A title no longer carries the site's name when the page's heading says it without ("… — Smashing Magazine"), and a title like "<details> …" keeps its first word.
- A link with a line break inside no longer falls apart into brackets.
- Links inside a code block are plain code.
- Code marked `brush: html` is highlighted as HTML.
- A link to a section written with underscores (`#technical_summary`) finds its heading.
- "Skip to main content" is no longer listed as the site's menu.
- A small picture among words — an avatar next to a name — stays in its line instead of taking a line with a caption.
- A date or a count ("13 October 2026", "321 comments") is no longer broken across two lines.
- On Android, a change of mobile network or system language no longer reloads the app.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak), `brevier-0.11.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.11.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key — it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.11.0-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.11.0-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), and `SHA256SUMS`.
