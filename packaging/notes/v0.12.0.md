Notes on a page, and pictures you can keep.

**Highlights and notes.** Select a passage and choose **Highlight** to mark it, or **Note** to write a few words next to it — in the text's menu in the window, in the selection menu next to Copy on the phone. A click or a tap on a marked passage shows its note and offers to edit or remove it. Open the page again and the marks are where you left them, even when the site has changed around them: a note keeps its quote and the words around it, not a position; one whose passage is gone stays on the list, marked as no longer on the page.

**Where to find them.** The shelf lists the page's notes above its contents, the bar under the text shows them as small squares next to the section dots, and **Your notes** in the menu (`brevier:notes`) lists all of them by page, with a link to your copy. A saved article carries its notes at the end, as quotes with your words under them, readable anywhere. A page with notes keeps its latest copy in the archive for good, as a bookmarked page does, and **Forget everything** leaves the notes alone. They live in `notes.tsv`, one line each.

**A click on a picture saves it.** It used to open the picture in another browser. Now a menu offers **Save image** — byte for byte as the site sent it, with the right extension even when a CDN serves WebP at an address ending in `.jpg` — and **Open in your browser**. On the phone the picture goes to `Pictures/Brevier` (no permission asked) or to **Share**, and nothing is downloaded again: the picture on the screen is the one that is kept.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak), `brevier-0.12.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.12.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key — it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.12.0-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.12.0-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), and `SHA256SUMS`.
