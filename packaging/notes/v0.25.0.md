Large print that fits the phone, saved pages that open with their pictures, and the start page now says which Brevier you have.

**Large print on the phone** (#65). Sizes go on past 22.5 pt: 24, 27, 30, 36, 42, 48, 56 and 64, so large print no longer depends on a zoom that lasts one run. On the phone the column is the screen, and at 250–300% the margins grew with the text and took a third of it. Now the text grows and the space around it doesn't: the column's margins, the indents of quotes and list items, and how much a heading is larger than the text stop growing at Brevier's own size. A formula or picture in a line shrinks to the width of its line instead of being cut off at the right edge. In the window the column is a line length in ems and grows by itself, so it is as it was; when a size does not fit the screen, the window now says that a shorter line length makes room.

**A saved `.zip` opens with its pictures.** Brevier saves a page with pictures as a `.zip` with an `images/` folder. A reader unpacked one and opened the `.md` on the phone, and every picture said "Could not make sense of the page": Android, and Flatpak through its portal, hand over the file alone, without the folder beside it. A saved `.zip` now opens as it is, without unpacking, in the window, on the command line and on the phone, and Brevier is offered in "Open with" for `.zip` files. Flatpak may now read your home folder, never write to it, so an unpacked `.md` there finds its pictures too; a page from the network still cannot reach your files. A picture that cannot be read says so, instead of blaming the page.

**Smaller things.** The start page ends with Brevier's version and a link to what's new in it: the version showed nowhere before. In the window a double click on the empty tab bar opens a new tab, as in other browsers. The waypoints of a long section on the shelf no longer come and go near the end of a page.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak; updates from Brevier's repository), `brevier-0.25.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.25.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key; it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.25.0-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.25.0-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), the command line alone: `brevier-cli-x86_64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-aarch64-linux.tar.gz`, `brevier-cli-macos.tar.gz` and `brevier-cli-windows-x86_64.zip`, and `SHA256SUMS`.
