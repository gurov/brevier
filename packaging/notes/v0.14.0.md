Habr comments under the article; the phone keeps your place while images load.

**Habr articles come with their comments.** Habr's article page carries no comments: its script fetches them once the page is open. Brevier now makes that same one request, only when the page says there are comments, and sets them under the article as a thread — every comment with its author and day, replies indented, a reply too deep to indent saying whom it answers. Each comment keeps Habr's own anchor, so a link to a comment, such as those in Habr's comment feed, opens at it, and the section is on the shelf. Deleted comments keep their place. A Habr comments page on its own reads as the thread. It costs one more request to habr.com for an article that has comments.

**The phone keeps your place while images load.** An image that loaded above the screen grew from its placeholder and pushed the text you were reading down: the app meant to hold the place, but never did. It now moves the page by exactly what the image grew, so returning to a page, following a link to a heading or a comment, or continuing where you left off stays where it should while the pictures arrive.
