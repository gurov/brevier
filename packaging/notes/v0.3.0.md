Feeds open in Brevier, and the window now draws from the same page model as the phone.

**Feeds.** An RSS or Atom address opens as a list of links: each entry's title, its date and author, and a couple of lines of its summary, in the feed's own order. RSS 2.0, RSS 1.0 and Atom are read, including feeds served under the wrong type, feeds whose encoding is named only in the XML declaration, and feeds that are not quite valid XML. A page that advertises feeds gets them on the shelf under "This site has a feed", in the window and on Android. `brevier <feed-url>` prints the list; `brevier --check` on a feed says it is served exactly.

**One layout for both front-ends.** The window draws articles from the core's page model, as the Android app does, so a change to how an article looks is made once. Nothing changes on screen — checked byte for byte over the corpus and pixel for pixel in the window — apart from one fix: a shelf entry for a paragraph or heading that begins with a formula no longer starts with a stray box (it showed on Android).

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak), `brevier-0.3.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.3.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key — it installs over 0.2.0), and `SHA256SUMS`.
