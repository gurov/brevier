A bolder mark.

**The icon.** The letter is heavier. At launcher size, in a list of apps and in Android's monochrome themed icons it no longer thins to a hairline, and its green-to-magenta gradient now reads at a glance. It is the same mark everywhere: the app on the phone, the window and its desktop entry on Linux, and the README.
