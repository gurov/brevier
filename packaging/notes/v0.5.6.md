Fixes for formulas, sharp images and the window icon. Nothing changes on the phone.

**Formulas on the line.** A formula with something below the line — a subscript, a bracket, a comma, a fraction — used to stand above the text around it, because the window put the bottom of the picture on the baseline. The formula's own depth below the line, which MathJax and Wikipedia write into the image, is now honoured: the formula sits on the line as in a browser, and the line makes room for what hangs below it.

**Sharp images on high-density screens.** On a screen with a scale factor of 2, pictures, diagrams and formulas were decoded at half the pixels they are shown with and came out soft. They are now decoded in the screen's own pixels, at the same size on the page; a small picture stays its own size. Moving the window to a screen of another density redraws the page.

**The window icon.** Where the window shows its icon in the header — the Flatpak build under GNOME and Ubuntu — the icon no longer sits flush against the window's edge.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak), `brevier-0.5.6-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.5.6-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key — it installs over earlier versions), and `SHA256SUMS`.
