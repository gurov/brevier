Search from the address bar, verse set as verse, and a start page that remembers.

**Search.** Anything typed in the address bar that is not an address — a few words, a question, one word without a dot — is a search: it goes to DuckDuckGo Lite, and the results come back as a list of links, the same view as a feed, each with its site and a couple of lines. The links lead straight to the sites, not through DuckDuckGo's click counter. A leading `?` searches for anything, `?danluu.com` included; `localhost` and `name:port` stay addresses. The command line searches the same way: `brevier "borrow checker"`. When DuckDuckGo takes a search for a bot and shows a puzzle instead of results, Brevier says so and offers to open the search in your browser.

**Verse.** A poem marked line by line — FictionBook's `<v>` and `<stanza>`, which many e-libraries keep in their HTML, or `verse`, `stanza` and `poem` classes — used to run together into one paragraph. Its lines now stay lines, stanzas are parted by an empty line, a line too long for the measure wraps with a hanging indent, and no line is hyphenated. A line break a page puts inside a paragraph (`<br>`) is kept too, where it used to become a space — except where a book scanned line by line put one at the end of every printed line: that prose is joined back into paragraphs, words broken at the line end included.

**The start page and Back.** A new tab shows the five pages you read last and a link to the whole history; on a first run it shows nothing of the kind. On a tab's first page Back is no longer greyed out: it leads to the start page. On Android the back gesture does the same, and from the start page it leaves the app as before.

**Long pages on the phone.** A whole novel on one page used to freeze the app for seconds before the first line appeared. Its first screen now shows at once, and the rest is laid out a piece at a time while you read.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak), `brevier-0.5.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.5.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key — it installs over earlier versions), and `SHA256SUMS`.
