Ready-made type, two lists instead of three, and a crash fixed.

**Ready-made type.** The Type section in Settings now starts with four styles: Brevier (as before), Book (your system's serif, 18 pt, justified), Large print (21 pt, shorter lines, more space between them) and Compact (15 pt, longer lines, more on the screen). The style your type matches is lit, and a line under the buttons says what it is. The single choices — face, size, line length, line spacing, justified text — are under **Fine-tune**, folded away unless your type is already your own. Brevier is the way back, so the Reset button is gone. In the window, a style that would not fit on the screen is refused, as a single choice is.

**Two lists instead of three.** The archive was the history and the bookmarks over again, with the text. Its menu item gives way to **Search what you've read** (`Ctrl+Shift+F`), and the results are titled "What you've read". The bookmarks now show each page's copy with its date, and say that a bookmark keeps it for good; the history says what "your copy" is. `brevier:archive` still lists every copy.

**Forget everything keeps the bookmarks' copies**, as it keeps the bookmarks.

**"Your copy" means the archive alone.** A page opened from the week's cache now says "Opened without downloading, saved 2 days ago".

**Fixed: typing an address on the Settings tab closed the window**, with every tab in it.

**Brevier's own pages are hyphenated** — the start page, the history and the bookmarks — so they read well justified too. The window's sample of your type no longer keeps the height of the previous one, and the phone's hyphenates as often as an article.

Files: `brevier.flatpak` (any distribution, with Flatpak), `brevier-0.10.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` (GTK 4 from the system), `brevier-0.10.0-arm64.apk` (Android 7 and later, 64-bit ARM, signed with the release key — it installs over earlier versions), `brevier-0.10.0-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` and `brevier-0.10.0-windows-x86_64.zip` (Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit), and `SHA256SUMS`.
