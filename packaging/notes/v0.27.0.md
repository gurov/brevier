The window prints a page in your type, the phone turns pages by tap zones the way e-book readers do, and a drawn formula is found, copied and selected like text.

**Print** (window). Ctrl+P, Print in the header menu, or a right click in the text. The sheet is set from the same page the window shows, in your face, line length, spacing and justification, at book size: the 16.5 pt screen text prints at 11 pt, a larger size larger. Links get small numbers, with their addresses listed at the end; highlights stay, and your notes follow the article. The first sheet names the address and the day, and every sheet is numbered. Lines and headings stay with what follows them, and a picture shrinks a little before it moves to the next sheet. "Print selection…" prints only what is selected, and Settings → Printing turns the addresses, the notes and the running heads off one by one. The phone does not print.

**Tap zones** (#66, phone). With "Tap to turn" on, the page is split the way e-book readers split it: a tap in the left third turns back (the right third with "Left hand" on), a tap in the middle shows the bar, and in full screen the system's bars with it, and a tap anywhere else turns forward. A narrow strip along each side edge, where the hand holding the phone rests, does nothing. The zones are shown when you turn taps on, and once more after this update. A turn now moves by a whole screen, like a page of a book: the first line not yet read comes to the top and no line is shown twice; back and then forward again returns to the same place. A tap on the text no longer nudges the page.

**Drawn formulas** (#16). Find on the page finds a fraction or a root that Brevier draws, by what it shows: `2` finds a formula with a 2 in it, while `the` does not find θ. On the phone a drawn formula now copies as its TeX source, `$\frac{1}{2}$`, as it already did in the window. A selection or a highlight now shows behind a drawn formula, in the window and on the phone, instead of a card of paper in the middle of it.

**Selection in the window**. With a clipboard manager that keeps the selection (Klipper on KDE Plasma), text selected with the mouse flickered and vanished while it was being dragged out. It now stays, and a middle click pastes clean text, the same as Ctrl+C.
